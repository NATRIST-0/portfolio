Technical Skills

After many years of studying science, I have developed a range of technical skills. Here are the most important ones:

Monitoring Scientific and Technical Developments

Monitor scientific and technical developments in the fields of climate and the environment by analysing relevant publications. (e.g., Literature review during internships)

Developing Environmental Assessments

Develop a climate or environmental assessment by drawing on various data sources and theoretical and practical knowledge. (e.g., Treatment and Analysis of Data from the O3 Reg Campaign)

Implementing Innovative Methodologies

Identify emerging scientific or technical issues in the fields of meteorology, oceanography and climate, and address them by implementing innovative numerical and instrumental methodologies (e.g., Development of innovative software in internship).

Conducting Measurement Campaigns

Plan, execute, and analyze comprehensive measurement campaigns across diverse industrial and research contexts, ensuring precision and adherence to required standards. Developed through practical work during my bachelor's (e.g., Vitamin C quantification project) and master's (oceanic and atmospheric measurements campaign).

Applying Metrology and Quality Processing

Implement metrological methods and quality control procedures to maintain reliability, traceability, and compliance in measurement systems and processes. This skill is a must, as a value is meaningless if not paired with an uncertainty.

Characterizing Physical and Chemical Quantities

Evaluate and analyze physical and chemical properties of materials using advanced characterization techniques (e.g., Ultra-Performance Liquid Chromatography - HPLC) to support development, quality control, and innovation.

Defining Measurement Specifications in an Environmental Context

Develop and implement measurement protocols aligned with environmental sustainability objectives, including monitoring and analyzing environmental parameters. Honed through experience with non-destructive testing methods.
